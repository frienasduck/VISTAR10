Chemical Reactions
and Equations

CBSE Class 10
13 Topics
High Weightage
Quick Summary

A chemical reaction is a process where reactants transform into products with new properties. We represent reactions through chemical equations — a shorthand using symbols and formulae. Every equation must be balanced to satisfy the Law of Conservation of Mass (atoms are neither created nor destroyed).

🔗
Combination Reaction
Two or more substances combine to form a single product. E.g., CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂. Always exothermic.
💥
Decomposition Reaction
A single compound breaks into two or more simpler substances. Requires energy (heat, light, electricity). Opposite of combination.
⚡
Displacement Reaction
A more reactive element displaces a less reactive one from its compound. E.g., Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu.
🔄
Double Displacement
Ions of two compounds exchange places forming two new compounds. Usually forms a precipitate. E.g., BaCl₂ + Na₂SO₄ → BaSO₄↓ + 2NaCl.
🔥
Oxidation & Reduction
Oxidation = gain of oxygen / loss of hydrogen. Reduction = loss of oxygen / gain of hydrogen. Both happen simultaneously (redox reaction).
🌫️
Corrosion & Rancidity
Corrosion = slow oxidation of metals (rusting of iron). Rancidity = oxidation of fats/oils causing bad smell. Both are harmful oxidation reactions.
⚗️ Key Equations to Remember
Combination
2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO  |  CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂
Decomposition
2Pb(NO₃)₂ → 2PbO + 4NO₂ + O₂  |  2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
Displacement
Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu  |  Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂
Dbl. Displacement
Na₂SO₄ + BaCl₂ → BaSO₄↓ + 2NaCl  |  AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl↓ + NaNO₃
